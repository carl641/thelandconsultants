Survey Pricing

Land Surveying Costs & Pricing Factors in Huntsville, AL

Because no two parcels in North Alabama are identical, land survey pricing in Huntsville depends on several specific project variables rather than a flat rate. Key factors influencing your survey cost include:

Property Size and Lot Boundary History

Larger acreage in rural Madison County requires more extensive field time than standard subdivision lots. Additionally, older properties with outdated deed records or missing historical boundary markers require additional research and retracement calculations.

Terrain and Accessibility

Huntsville’s unique geography—ranging from steep, forested slopes on Monte Sano Mountain to the low-lying floodplains along the Tennessee River—impacts field navigation and data collection time.

Type of Survey Required

A standard Residential Boundary Survey for a fence line or property line dispute requires fewer resources than a full ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey or an elevation certificate for flood insurance compliance.

Our Process

How Our Huntsville Surveying Process Works

From initial boundary research to physical field staking, we follow a rigorous, state-certified process to deliver clear, accurate property lines:

1

Research & Records Analysis

Before field deployment, our team conducts thorough legal deed research, examining historical Madison County parcel maps, recorded plats, and GIS data to project your property’s exact monument locations.

2

Advanced Fieldwork & Boundary Locating

Utilizing precision equipment—including RTK GPS receivers, Total Station instruments, and aerial drone LiDAR for complex terrain—our field crews locate existing physical monuments and measure distances and angles with exact precision.

3

Monuments, Staking & Deliverables

If original property pins are missing or obscured, our licensed surveyors calculate and set new permanent iron monuments in accordance with Alabama standards. We place visible temporary boundary stakes along property lines and identify all utility and drainage easements impacting your land, providing you with an official recorded survey drawing.
